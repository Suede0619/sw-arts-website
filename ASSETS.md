# Original prototype imagery

All five raster images were created with the built-in image-generation tool for this project on October 19, 2026. These are original illustrative prototype assets, not depictions of actual listed venues, not work by the researched artists, and not evidence of any physical object being available. No third-party artwork or venue photograph has been republished.

Final website assets are saved in **`dist/assets/`** as WebP files. Card derivatives are 760 pixels wide; detail/hero derivatives are up to 1,600 pixels wide, preserving the artwork. Original PNGs were copied into the project-local ignored `source-assets/` directory; the generated originals also remain in the generator's default storage. `cwebp` performed ordinary resizing/encoding only. No artwork was semantically edited or recolored.

`geometry.svg`, `lines.svg` and `favicon.svg` are original code-native vector designs. The region schematic is an original diagram, explicitly not to scale or for navigation.

## Final prompts used with the built-in tool

### `gallery-hero.webp`

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide editorial hero photograph for an art discovery website prototype. Create an original photorealistic high-end contemporary art gallery interior inspired by northern New Mexico adobe architecture: warm ivory plaster walls, very high wood beam ceiling, warm sunlight falling across polished pale concrete, a single large original abstract painting in clay red, ultramarine and cream on the far wall, one quiet sculptural bench. A doorway reveals distant blue mountain ridges and high desert grasses. No people, no signs, no logos, no text. Beautiful restrained architectural photography, sophisticated art direction, real material texture, warm natural light, quiet and welcoming rather than ostentatious. Wide landscape composition 3:2. This is a fictional illustrative gallery; do not depict or imitate a named real gallery or any living artist's artwork.

Sophisticated original composition, no copied artist style, no signature, no lettering, no frame, straight-on flat artwork reproduction, landscape aspect ratio 4:3, edge-to-edge painting.
